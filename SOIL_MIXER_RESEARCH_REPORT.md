## Notes on Disqualified Prospects

During research, the following types were excluded:
- Pure commodity topsoil/mulch yards with no organic positioning
- Equipment manufacturers (Royer Industries, FEECO)
- Garden centers without blending operations
- Retail-only operations with no wholesale capability
- Municipal compost facilities (unless commercial)
