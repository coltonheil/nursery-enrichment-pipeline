## Cold Email Template

**Subject Lines (A/B test these):**
- "Quick question about [Company]'s [relevant process]"
- "Reducing waste in [industry] operations"
- "How [Competitor] improved their sustainability"

**Body:**

```
Hi [First Name],

I noticed [Company] is focused on [relevant initiative from research - e.g., sustainability, cost reduction]. 

At WormCo, we help [industry] businesses turn organic waste into revenue through vermicomposting. Companies like [relevant example] have:
- Reduced waste disposal costs by 40-60%
- Generated additional revenue streams
- Achieved sustainability goals faster

Would a 15-minute call next week make sense to explore if this could work for [Company]?

Best,
[Your Name]
WormCo
[Phone] | [Email]
```

**Notes:**
- Personalize first line with specific research
- Use industry-specific pain points
- Keep under 100 words
- Single clear CTA

## Follow-Up Sequence

### Follow-Up #1 (3 days after initial)

**Subject:** Re: [Original subject]

```
Hi [First Name],

Following up on my note below about [specific benefit]. 

I realize timing might not be perfect, but I'd love to share:
- A 2-minute overview of how [similar company] implemented this
- Specific cost/benefit projections for [Company]

Worth a quick call?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Follow-Up #2 (5 days after FU#1)

**Subject:** [Resource] for [Company]

```
Hi [First Name],

Thought this might be useful even if we don't connect:

[Attach one-pager or case study]

This shows how [industry] companies are approaching [pain point]. If you'd like to discuss how it applies to [Company], I'm happy to walk through it.

No pressure if timing isn't right.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Follow-Up #3 (7 days after FU#2) - Breakup Email

**Subject:** Should I close your file?

```
Hi [First Name],

I haven't heard back, so I'm assuming this isn't a priority right now.

I'll close out my note on [Company] unless you'd like me to:
- Follow up in [Q2/Q3]
- Send info to a different contact
- Share resources without a call

Let me know, otherwise I'll stop bothering you!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Notes:**
- Stop after breakup email unless they respond
- Log response (or lack of) in CRM
- Mark for future re-engagement if appropriate

## Phone Script (for inbound/warm leads)

### Opening

```
Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] from WormCo. Thanks for taking my call.

I know your time is valuable, so I'll keep this brief. The goal today is to understand:
1. What your current waste management looks like
2. If vermicomposting could help [Company] save money or hit sustainability goals
3. If it makes sense to explore further

Sound good?

[If yes, continue. If no, ask when would be better]
```

### Discovery Questions

1. "Walk me through your current organic waste stream - what types and volumes?"
2. "What's your current disposal method and cost?"
3. "Any sustainability or waste reduction goals on your radar?"
4. "Who else should be involved in evaluating something like this?"
5. "What would success look like for you?"

### Qualification

**Good Fit Signals:**
- >500 lbs/week organic waste
- Paying for disposal currently
- Sustainability initiatives active
- Interest in cost reduction
- Decision-maker or influencer on call

**Poor Fit Signals:**
- Very low volume (<100 lbs/week)
- No budget/authority to explore
- Already using competing solution
- No pain point expressed

### Next Steps

```
Based on what you've shared, I think there's potential here. 

Next step would be:
- [Site assessment / waste audit]
- [Custom cost-benefit analysis]
- [Pilot program proposal]

Would [day/time] work to [next action]?

[If yes: confirm and send calendar invite]
[If no: when would work better?]
```

### Closing

```
Great, I'll [send materials / schedule follow-up / conduct assessment].

You should receive [deliverable] by [date]. Any questions before we wrap up?

Thanks for your time, [First Name]. Looking forward to [next step].
```

## Objection Handling

### "We don't have budget right now"

**Response:**
"I understand. The good news is most implementations are cost-neutral or positive within 6-12 months because of waste disposal savings. Would it make sense to model the ROI first, then revisit budget timing?"

**Goal:** Move to analysis phase without commitment

---

### "We already have a waste solution"

**Response:**
"That's great - what are you using? [Listen] How's it working for you? [Listen for pain points] The reason I ask is that many of our customers were already doing something but found vermicomposting added [specific benefit they're missing]."

**Goal:** Identify gaps in current solution

---

### "I need to talk to [other stakeholders]"

**Response:**
"Absolutely. Would it help if I joined that conversation to answer technical questions? Or would you prefer I send materials you can share?"

**Goal:** Get access to decision-makers or provide tools for internal champion

---

### "This seems complicated"

**Response:**
"Fair concern. The actual implementation is usually simpler than it sounds. Let me walk you through [specific simple example]. Most of our customers are operational within 2-4 weeks with minimal training."

**Goal:** Provide concrete simplicity evidence

---

### "How do I know this will work for us?"

**Response:**
"Great question. That's exactly why we start with [assessment/pilot]. We'll analyze your specific waste stream, model the economics, and if it doesn't make sense, we'll tell you. No point moving forward if the numbers don't work."

**Goal:** Low-risk next step, build trust

## Customization Guide

**Industry-Specific Hooks:**

- **Restaurants/Food Service:** "Food waste → fertilizer → local farms loop"
- **Grocery/Retail:** "Shrinkage reduction + sustainability marketing"
- **Manufacturing:** "Organic byproduct → revenue stream"
- **Education:** "Hands-on sustainability education + cost savings"
- **Agriculture:** "On-site fertility production from organic waste"

**Company Size Messaging:**

- **Small (<50 employees):** Focus on simplicity, cost savings, DIY option
- **Medium (50-500):** Balance of cost + sustainability + ease
- **Large (500+):** Scale, brand reputation, ESG reporting, pilot programs
